[ 06 · Personal interests ]

What inspires me beyond the code .

Kandel is more than a place to work. It is where I play, organise events, take to the stage and have put down roots.

“Turm Kandel” chess club

Thinking through variations

Whether on Fridays at the after-school club or in competitions at the council chamber: chess is not about rushing to move pieces. It is about calmly analysing positions, thinking through plans and keeping an overview even under time pressure. I play actively for the Turm Kandel chess club.

Grenzüberschreitende Freundschaften e.V.

Friendships across borders

Before moving to Kandel, I had never come across this kind of association, but I immediately liked the idea: it maintains the town’s twinning partnerships with Reichshoffen in Alsace and Whitworth in England. I especially enjoy meeting our English friends — their mentality just suits me. Those encounters have led to friendships that have now lasted 15 years. Because I believe in the idea so strongly, I decided early on to get involved on the committee.

Bienwald-Karneval-Gesellschaft (Bi-Ka-Ge)

From silliness to Swiss precision

For years I performed with the Showbuwe, covering the full range with the group — from complete silliness to clever insider humour. These days, I take the stage as a carnival speaker. My task: with a Swiss accent, dialect and meticulous attention to detail, I lead the audience in the opening oath to the “goldich Kannler Fasenacht”, Kandel’s carnival. My fondness for dry British humour comes in very handy.

Roots & home in Kandel
